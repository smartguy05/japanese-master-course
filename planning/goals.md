# Project Goals - Nihongo Master

## Vision Statement

**Create the most effective AI-powered Japanese learning platform that takes absolute beginners to business-level proficiency (N2-N3) through engaging, personalized, conversational practice.**

---

## Primary Objectives

### 1. Educational Effectiveness
**Goal:** Enable learners to achieve N3 proficiency within 12-18 months of consistent daily use (30 min/day)

**Success Criteria:**
- [ ] Users can pass JLPT N5 practice tests after 3 months
- [ ] Users can pass JLPT N4 practice tests after 6 months
- [ ] Users can pass JLPT N3 practice tests after 12 months
- [ ] Users can hold 10-minute conversations about everyday topics
- [ ] Users can read and comprehend basic business emails

**Key Results (Measurable):**
- 80%+ of active users advance one JLPT level within 6 months
- Average conversation length increases 50% month-over-month
- Reading comprehension accuracy >85% for level-appropriate content
- Vocabulary retention rate >75% for SRS-reviewed words

## Anti-Goals (What We're NOT Trying to Do)

### ❌ Not a Social Network
- We're not building social features, follower systems, or public profiles
- Focus is individual learning, not community engagement
- No user-generated content moderation challenges

### ❌ Not a Marketplace
- We're not selling courses, lessons, or paid add-ons
- No instructor marketplace or third-party content sellers
- Single, comprehensive learning path

### ❌ Not Gamified to Addiction
- We want healthy daily practice, not compulsive usage
- No dark patterns, infinite scrolling, or engagement manipulation
- Healthy boundaries: 30-60 min/day is ideal, not 3+ hours

### ❌ Not a Translation Tool
- We're teaching Japanese, not providing translation services
- No translation API or dictionary-only mode
- Focus on learning, not quick lookups

### ❌ Not Replacing Human Tutors
- AI augments learning but doesn't replace human interaction
- Encourage users to practice with real people
- No claims of "better than human teachers"

## Definition of Done

**The project is "done" when:**

1. ✅ A complete beginner can reach N3 proficiency in 12-18 months
2. ✅ Users report >4.0/5.0 satisfaction with learning outcomes
3. ✅ Platform is stable, secure, and well-documented
4. ✅ Docker deployment works reliably for self-hosting
5. ✅ All core features (alphabet, kanji, vocabulary, grammar, conversation, speech) are fully functional
6. ✅ Content is accurate, comprehensive, and culturally appropriate
7. ✅ AI conversations feel natural and educational
8. ✅ Users successfully prepare for Japanese business environments
9. ✅ Technical debt is minimal and maintainability is high
10. ✅ The platform can run successfully for years with minimal intervention
